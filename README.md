# ⚡ Fatloss Diet Plan Creator (English Breakfasts & Kerala Meals Edition)

A clean, modern, and user-friendly web application designed to generate a simple, effective 7-day fat loss diet plan. It combines high-protein **English Breakfasts**, authentic portion-controlled **Kerala Meals**, fresh **Raw Salads**, and a dedicated **Grocery Shopping Checklist**.

100% runs directly in your browser with **zero logins, zero backend, and zero external services**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **User-Friendly & Jargon-Free**:
   - Replaced complex terms with clear, direct numbers: **Daily Calories**, **Daily Water**, **Daily Protein**, and **Weight Goal**.
   - Streamlined 3 main meals daily:
     - 🍳 **Breakfast** (English Classics & Kerala Favorites)
     - 🍛 **Kerala Lunch** (Portion-controlled Matta Rice & Fish/Chicken/Dal Curries)
     - 🥗 **Daily Fresh Salad** (Mandatory raw salad 10 minutes before lunch or dinner)
     - 🍲 **Light Dinner** (Pan-grilled fish, egg bhurji, or oats broth with salad)
     - Plus a clean, non-intrusive banner for morning and evening tea/snacks.

2. **No Malayalam Script (100% English)**:
   - Everything is written in clean, easy-to-read English.

3. **Dedicated Grocery Shopping Purchases Section**:
   - Located on the main page.
   - Categorized into 5 simple groups:
     1. Grains, Cereals & Breads
     2. Fresh Vegetables & Greens
     3. Proteins, Fish & Dairy
     4. Lentils, Seeds & Legumes
     5. Kerala Spices & Healthy Pantry
   - **Progress Tracker**: See how many items you've checked off.
   - **Add Custom Item**: Easily type any extra item and add it to your checklist.
   - **Quick Actions**: "Copy Shopping List" (formatted for WhatsApp) and "Print Checklist".

4. **Interactive Controls**:
   - Change individual meals using the **Swap** button.
   - Regenerate the full week anytime.
   - Track daily water glasses with a single tap.
   - Save your progress automatically in browser memory.
